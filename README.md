🌸 Floral Haven
>>>> 🌷 A Beautiful & Simple Flower Shop Website

   > **Floral Haven** is a frontend flower shop website designed to bring the beauty of flowers into a simple and elegant online experience.  
   > Users can explore different flowers, bouquets, flower pots, view product details, read reviews, and contact the shop.

>>✨ Features

🌸 **Beautiful Home Page**  
A welcoming landing page with a flower-themed design.
💐 **Flower Collection**  
Browse different flowers, bouquets, and flower pots.
🔍 **Product Details**  
Every product has its own detail page with more information.
⭐ **Customer Reviews**  
A dedicated review section where customers can see and submit reviews.
📩 **Contact Page**  
A simple contact form for users to get in touch.
📖 **About Us**  
Learn more about Floral Haven and the idea behind the website.
🎥 **Flower Video**  
An additional visual section with a flower-related video.
📱 **Responsive Layout**  
The website layout is designed to work on different screen sizes.
